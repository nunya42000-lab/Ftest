Machine tone capture

Records the tones your machine plays, so Follow Me can recognise them without every user calibrating first.

  1. Put the phone close to the machine's speaker — closer than feels necessary. Tones need to be clearly louder than the room or the quiet ones are never heard at all.
  2. Turn on the microphone and let it measure the room. Don't drag the gate below the figure it reports.
  3. Start recording, then tap the matching number on the keypad as each tone plays — including the tones you play back yourself.
  4. Several full rounds is ideal. Stop, then send the result back.
